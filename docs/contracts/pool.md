# Native pool contract
The key words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **REQUIRED**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, and **MAY** express requirement levels as defined in [RFC 2119](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119.txt). Operator use: [Native pool operation](../pool.md).

## Clauses
### `POOL-01`: Explicit enablement and operator configuration
- Off by default; the `pool` feature MUST NOT enable the runtime implicitly (SV2 additionally requires `pool-sv2`). Enabling MUST require a nonempty pool datadir, at least one explicit listener, an exact plain-decimal share difficulty, a positive work window, a reward script, plus a node mining payout address; node payout address and ledger reward script MUST be consistent and the ledger MUST NOT infer ownership or redirect a coinbase. Ports MUST be nonzero; SV1/SV2 addresses MUST differ; non-loopback binds MUST require `pool_allow_non_loopback` and omission MUST NOT imply permission. SV2 bind and authority-file path MUST be paired; sub-options MUST NOT be silently ignored when disabled. The node MUST reject maturity below 100, zero payout confirmations, fees above 10,000 basis points, and a reorg watch shorter than maturity, payout-confirmation depth, or the fixed 6-confirmation abandon depth. Reward scripts MUST be nonempty, at most 10,000 bytes, minimally encoded, and not `OP_RETURN`; passing these checks MUST NOT be represented as proof of spendability or control.
### `POOL-02`: Schema consent, storage, and immutable policy
- The additive `PoolLedger` family has explicit approval, which does not authorize deployment or existing-data cutover. Verification MUST use disposable stores (including old-schema fixtures) and MUST NOT open or migrate operator databases. The pool MUST use the selected backend through the storage-owned `KvStore` boundary and MUST NOT take a direct engine dependency or implicitly reset a database. The service MUST lock the dedicated pool directory, create a ledger only when absent, and reopen only with the persisted immutable policy equal; policy changes MUST NOT reinterpret history and a mismatch MUST fail rather than delete or recreate. Creation/opening plus run-epoch allocation MUST precede admission; the epoch MUST separate restarted protocol job identities.
### `POOL-03`: Shared admission and bounded deferred work
- SV1 and SV2 MUST share one total/per-IP limiter (defaults 128 total, 8 per IP); both limits MUST be positive and per-IP MUST NOT exceed total. Retention MUST use the limits owned by `crates/node/src/pool.rs`: `MAX_RETAINED_JOBS`, `SESSION_SLOTS_PER_CONNECTION`, and `MAX_SHARES_PER_JOB`; owner sessions and TCP connections are distinct budgets. The deferred share-ledger outbox MUST use `MAX_OUTBOX_RECORDS` and `MAX_OUTBOX_BYTES` with exhaustion rejecting pre-validation, and SV1 MUST withhold success until accounting completion. Found-block processing MUST reserve capacity (8 slots shared by reservations and recovery records, 4,000,000 bytes per slot) before feed/validation emits record-requiring work; failed writes MUST keep the compact projection, not full blocks. Per-connection limits stay in force; no single bound claims a total-process memory bound.
### `POOL-04`: Authority-key handling
- The SV2 authority MUST be a matching BIP340 x-only public/secret pair; it authenticates transport and MUST NOT be treated as payout custody. On Unix the file MUST be a regular file with bits exactly `0600`, opened `RDONLY|CLOEXEC|NOFOLLOW|NONBLOCK` with descriptor-metadata validation; a final-component symlink or special file MUST NOT bypass the check. Content MUST be exactly two ASCII-whitespace-separated 64-hex-digit fields (public first, secret second); input over 256 bytes or extra fields MUST be rejected. Non-Unix MUST fail closed (unsupported operation). File, decoded-secret, and stored-secret buffers MUST use zeroizing ownership, `Debug` MUST redact the secret, and parse errors MUST NOT include key input (this does not certify dependency/kernel/transient copies). Operators SHOULD protect ancestor directories; `NOFOLLOW` MUST NOT be described as an ancestor-directory guarantee.
### `POOL-05`: One mining owner and acknowledgement boundaries
- Both protocols MUST derive jobs and validate work through the shared `MiningControl`-backed owner and MUST NOT add a second template/consensus validator. Found blocks MUST submit through node mining control before the accounting record finalizes; the outcome MUST NOT substitute for active-chain evidence. After `Applied`, the active job MUST retire synchronously (`invalidate_active` before ledger/outbox/ack); work on a retired job is stale while a byte-identical replay keeps duplicate identity (SV1 21 vs 22; SV2 `stale-share` vs `duplicate-share`) and MUST NOT receive a second credit. An exact `inconclusive-not-best-prevblk` receipt on an in-flight sibling MUST complete its durable record then answer nonfatal stale; any other rejection MUST NOT be answered stale. SV2 `SubmitShares.Success` MUST wait for host confirmation of the durable outcome (an idempotent record MAY satisfy it; queued/failed MUST NOT present as committed). SV1 success MUST wait for a completion token covering the share write (plus block-record write for found blocks); failed inline writes MAY sit in the bounded outbox but queueing MUST NOT complete the response. Each SV1 session MUST keep at most one pending accounting response with extras shed pre-validation as retryable, polling only after completion; work lost before completion MUST NOT already have received success. Found records MUST start pending and deduplicate by block/share identity; retries MUST NOT double-allocate. Parents, clean-job flags, and receipts MUST NOT serve as reorg/maturity evidence.
### `POOL-06`: PPLNS reward and maturity
- Rounds MUST snapshot the work-weighted accepted-share window at the solving share (not connection/account/share counts). Fixing MUST require authoritative canonical observation of the exact block and coinbase with agreeing txid and value; missing bodies or contradictions MUST NOT fabricate a reward. Gross MUST equal observed coinbase outputs paying the pool script, the fee MUST be floor-rounded integer basis points, and allocations MUST conserve net. Balances MUST credit only after the configured maturity with confirmations `tip_height - block_height + 1`, threshold at or above 100. Admission MUST require a registered payout script; decimal account IDs MAY carry one nonempty worker suffix; login syntax MUST NOT be represented as ownership proof.
### `POOL-07`: Hash-keyed reorg reconciliation and conservation
- Membership MUST be observed by exact block hash with height as lookup hint only; known displaced hashes MUST stay distinguishable from unknown hashes even when the replacement tip is lower or equal. The adapter MUST take stable applied-chain observations and check tx/spend index hints against the canonical body before accepting payout evidence. A displaced matured round MUST orphan and reverse allocations, consuming available funds first with the remainder an explicit deficit (never erasing a reservation or rewriting paid amounts). Future credits MUST clear deficits before increasing available funds, keeping `available + reserved + paid - deficit` equal to credited matured allocations. Unknown membership MUST NOT prove canonicality or displacement; monitoring is bounded by the watch horizon and MUST NOT be advertised as unlimited deep-reorg recovery. Payout reversal MUST preserve reservation/accounting transitions; mempool absence MUST NOT prove unusable signatures or unspendable inputs.
### `POOL-08`: External signing without wallet custody
- Pool RPC MUST stay authenticated and key-free; the pool MUST NOT generate, import, expose, or use payout private keys while the external signer owns funding, input selection, signatures, and fees. `preparepayoutbatch` MUST reserve with a 32-byte idempotency key and MUST NOT lower the minimum payout (floor 546 satoshis through `MAX_MONEY`) or exceed the output cap (absolute maximum 2500). Requests MUST use strict canonical BRPR v1 and receipts BRPT v1 with big-endian integers (BRPR: exact outputs plus authorized change scripts; BRPT: batch ID, txid, raw transaction). Receipts MUST recompute the txid, match the exact payout-output multiset, reject unauthorized extras and invalid/duplicate inputs, and preserve reservations on rejection; replacements MUST conflict with earlier signed transactions rather than double-pay. At most one authorized change output is allowed and the node policy supplies none, so node-composed batches MUST reject all extra change. Receipt checks MUST NOT be described as signature/ownership verification; `recordpayoutreceipt` MUST NOT broadcast, with chain observation of the txid as acceptance evidence and payment confirmation requiring the configured depth. Failures, timeouts, and rejections MUST NOT implicitly release reservations; cancellation MUST require a prepared batch plus an explicit `never_handed_to_signer`/`signer_revoked` basis that the operator establishes. Missing txindex/script-index observer errors MUST NOT become nonpayment or safe-abandonment evidence.
### `POOL-09`: Lifecycle and protocol scope
- Composition MUST build the ledger/operator handle before RPC publication, and listeners MUST start only after RPC bind plus an initial template. Reconciliation MUST NOT hold shutdown hostage to a future generation; shutdown MUST stop admission, cancel and join workers, and drain bounded deferred work within the deadline, with a miss or panic as error, not success. SV2 is limited to the `sv2.rs` role matrix (Noise responder, setup v2, standard/extended channels, per-connection group, job/share handling plus reconnect). Custom jobs/work selection, Job Declaration, Template Distribution server, extranonce-prefix changes, and extensions negotiation MUST NOT be advertised; the in-process template source MUST NOT be confused with a Template Distribution server.
## Evidence boundary

Current execution results belong to CI or the PR. In-tree protocol/ledger evidence does not certify an external miner or the entire tree. Existing operator databases MUST remain untouched; deployment and existing-data cutover require their separate approval.
