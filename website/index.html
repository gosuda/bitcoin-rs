Inspect the constraint register ↗

Kernel-free is a scoped statement.
The documented default binary excludes libbitcoinkernel. Library and container defaults differ; other native dependencies still need review.

Historical does not mean current.
The benchmark owner retains bounded results from superseded engines and marks end-state cells planned_not_executed. This page makes no current speed claim.

A first run is a first run.
A startup or RPC response does not prove full consensus behavior, full-tip sync, recovery, or production safety.

The developer challenge

One Node. One Result.

Make your evaluation useful to the next developer. A reproducible non-security failure is valuable too.

01

Choose a local path.

Use a separate regtest environment. Record the source revision, platform, build features, and exact commands. Do not use real funds or production data.

02

Capture what happened.

Record actual output—not a reconstructed success screenshot. Explain what the result tests, what failed, and what remains untested.

03

Contribute one finding.

Share a precise, redacted report or improvement through the contribution process. For potential vulnerabilities, confirm a private maintainer channel before sharing technical details; do not open a public report.

Find your contribution path ↗

Record successes, failures, and untested behavior separately. First-run timings are not benchmark evidence.
